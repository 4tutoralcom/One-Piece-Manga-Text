THE SLICING WINDS OF ROMMEL.

TERRIFYING GUSTS OF WIND THAT SLASHED RIGHT THROUGH INNOCENT BYSTANDERS! A NAVAL BATTLESHIP WAS DEPLOYED TO SOLVE THE ISSUE.

YEARS AGO, THERE WERE REPORTS OF A STRANGE PHNON OCCURRING IN THE KINGDOM OF ROMMEL LATE AT NIGHT...

WE STILL HAVEN'T BROUGHT HIM TO JUSTICE...

IT WAS A MAN NAMED HAKUBA.

...BUT IN THE END, WE LEARNED THE CAUSE OF THOSE CUITTING BREEZES.

WE LOST A LOT OF GOOD SAILORS TO THAT EERIE WIND...

...BUT EVERY TIMEHAKUBA APPEARS, ANOTHER PIRATE HAPPENS TO BE IN TOWN AT THE SAME TIME.
